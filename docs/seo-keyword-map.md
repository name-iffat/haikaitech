## Keyword watchlist (Trends MY, 12mo — relative index, checked 2026-11-16)

High volume, price intent: `harga website`, `buat website`, `cara buat website`,
`website murah`. Low volume, high intent: `web design penang`, `web design
selangor`, `web designer selangor price`. DIY lean: `cara buat website sendiri`,
`buat website free`, `wix`. Rule: price content chases volume, geo pages
capture intent, DIY content pivots to done-for-you.
