# Website Cost Malaysia: Ranking First Gets You Fewer Clicks Now

This post was sparked by an Ahrefs short, ["Google borrowed a 1922 idea"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zW8ZndIH0IU). Their point: back in 1922, Reader's Digest built a magazine out of condensed versions of other magazines. Google's AI Overviews do the same thing to your web pages today. And they put a number on it. Across 300,000 keywords, Ahrefs found the number one result gets 58% fewer clicks when an AI Overview shows up. That figure comes from their updated study published in February 2026, measured with Google Search Console data. I am quoting their research, not mine.

Read that again. You can win first place on Google and still lose more than half the clicks.

## Ranking was the goal. Now it is only half the job.

For years the sales pitch for websites and SEO was simple. Rank first, get the clicks, get the customers. That deal is breaking. Google answers the question right on the results page now, so plenty of people never click anything. I wrote before about traffic being something you cannot fully control in [Website Traffic Malaysia](/blog/website-traffic-malaysia-platform-dependence/). This is the same story one step further. Even the traffic you earn by ranking gets taxed by the AI box above you.

So here is my honest take. If you are paying for a website mainly to chase rank-one clicks, you are buying yesterday's game. The website is still worth paying for. But what you are paying for has changed.

## People still click when they are checking you out

Not every search triggers an AI answer. The searches that still produce clicks are the ones with intent behind them. Someone heard your business name from a friend. Someone saw your booth at an expo. Someone got your WhatsApp broadcast and wants to verify you are real. They type your name into Google. Google shows your site. They click to check your services, your prices, and how to reach you.

That visitor is worth ten casual browsers. The whole job of your website is to turn that one visit into one WhatsApp message. Nothing else matters as much.

## What to demand from a website at this point

Forget rank tracking reports for a minute. Ask these questions about any site you pay for.

Does it say what you sell and roughly what it costs within seconds? If a visitor has to dig through three pages to find a price or a quote button, the AI box already won. It answered faster than you.

Can a visitor message you in one tap from any page? A WhatsApp button that is always visible beats a contact form every time for Malaysian SMEs. People here do not fill forms. They chat.

Does your business name search show your site first with correct info? This is the one ranking battle still fully worth winning. Your own name. Free to check, just Google it.

Is your key info written plainly enough that AI answers quote it correctly? Hours, areas served, services, contact number. If Google is going to summarise you anyway, make sure the source material is right.

I covered the enquiry side of this in [Lead Generation for SME Malaysia](/blog/lead-generation-malaysia-sme/). Traffic without follow-up is a leaking pipe. Now the pipe itself got narrower, so every drop counts more.

## Common questions

### Does ranking number one still matter?

Less than before, for general questions Google answers directly. It still matters for searches with buying intent and for your own business name. Win those.

### Will AI Overviews kill small business websites?

No. They mostly eat informational clicks, people looking for quick answers. They do not replace a customer checking your prices and messaging you. That part still needs your site.

### Should I stop doing SEO then?

I would not stop. I would stop obsessing over informational keywords and put the effort into pages that convert. Service pages, price info, area pages, and a fast path to WhatsApp.

### Can AI answers show wrong info about my business?

Yes, and that is a real risk. AI summaries pull from whatever is out there, including outdated directory listings. Keeping your own site accurate gives Google a correct source to quote.
