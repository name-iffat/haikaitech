## The pain it removes

When you buy a one-time website, your ongoing responsibilities are: renew the domain, renew the hosting, keep SSL valid, apply security updates, monitor downtime, back up content, and — every time you want a small change — email a developer and pay per edit.

That's a lot of mental load for an owner who just wants to run their business. The managed plan takes all of it off your plate for **RM199/month**, with a 12-month minimum and **no large upfront payment**.

## What "small content updates" means

Included are the everyday edits — updating opening hours, swapping a photo, changing a phone number, tweaking a paragraph, adding a menu item. Larger work (a new page, a new language, a blog) is quoted separately at the published [add-on rates](/services/).

The plan runs on a **12-month minimum**. After that it continues month-to-month, and you can stop anytime. There's no lock-in on the *site itself*: it's your content, and you can take it with you if you leave.
