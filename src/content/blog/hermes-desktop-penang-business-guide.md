## If you have never heard of it

Hermes Desktop is an AI assistant app for your computer, built by Nous Research. Think of it as a capable helper that lives on your machine: you type requests in plain language, and it does computer work — drafting, organising, researching, remembering context across conversations.

It is not a chatbot on a website. It runs on your own computer, connects to a model provider of your choice, and can be given instructions about you and your business so its answers get more useful over time.

## What a Penang SME actually uses it for

- **Client messages** — draft replies to enquiries in your tone, then review before sending. You stay in charge of what goes out.
- **Research notes** — turn a messy pile of links and PDFs into a one-page brief before a supplier meeting.
- **Invoice and email drafts** — structured starting points from your notes, checked by you before use.
- **Business memory** — teach it your services, prices and standard answers once, and stop repeating yourself.

Everything it drafts gets checked by a human before use. That rule is the whole difference between a useful assistant and an embarrassing incident.
