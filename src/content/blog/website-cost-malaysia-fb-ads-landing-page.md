# Website Cost Malaysia: Your Ads Are Fine, Your Landing Page Is the Leak

This post was sparked by a MoreMozi short, ["A Lower Return on Ad Spend Can Still Leave You With More Money"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bgQ-9n_Our8). The point is simple. A lower return percentage on a bigger spend can still put more cash in your pocket than a great return on a tiny spend. I want to translate that for Malaysian SMEs running Facebook and TikTok ads, because most of you are making the opposite mistake. You protect a pretty ROAS number and stay small.

## A simple example with round numbers

This is my own simplified illustration, not data from anyone. Assume your margin after product cost is around half, just to keep the math easy.

You spend RM500 on ads and get RM2,500 in sales. That is a 5x return. Sounds great. After product cost you keep about RM1,250, minus the RM500 in ads, so roughly RM750 stays with you.

Now you spend RM2,000 and get RM6,000 in sales. That is only a 3x return. Worse on paper. But after product cost you keep about RM3,000, minus RM2,000 in ads, so roughly RM1,000 stays with you.

The uglier number made you more money. That is the whole idea in the video. Judge ads by the ringgit left over, not by the ratio.

## Why this breaks for most SMEs here

The math above assumes every extra visitor buys at roughly the same rate. For most Malaysian SMEs, that is not true. And the reason is not the ads. It is where the ads send people.

Most of you send paid traffic straight to a WhatsApp link. No page. No prices. No proof you are real. Or you send it to an old website with last year's phone number and photos that load sideways on a phone. So the first RM500 finds your warmest buyers and converts fine. Then you scale up, colder strangers arrive, they check you out, they find nothing convincing, and they vanish. Your cost per enquiry climbs and you conclude ads do not scale. Ads scaled. Your closer did not.

I wrote about the enquiry side of this in [Lead Generation for SME Malaysia](/blog/lead-generation-malaysia-sme/). Traffic without follow-up is a leaking pipe. Paid traffic into a page that cannot close is the same leak, just more expensive water.

## Fix the closer before you scale the spend

You do not need a big website for this. You need one page that does four jobs. It says what you sell in plain words. It shows a rough price range so serious buyers are not scared off. It shows some proof, past work or a testimonial or even just a real address. And it has a WhatsApp button that is always one tap away with the message already typed.

That is it. A page like that turns your ad spend from a gamble into something you can scale without fear.

## Common questions

### My ROAS dropped when I raised my budget. Should I cut back?

Not automatically. Check the ringgit left over after product cost and ad spend. If that number still grew, the lower ratio is fine. Cut back only when the extra spend stops paying for itself.

### What ROAS should a Malaysian SME aim for?

There is no universal number. It depends on your margin. A food seller with thin margins needs a higher return than a contractor with fat margins. Work backwards from your own costs instead of copying someone else's target.

### Do I need a website if my ads go straight to WhatsApp?

WhatsApp closes, but something has to sell before the chat. Strangers from ads check you out first. A simple page that proves you are legit raises the share of clickers who actually message you. That is what makes scaling possible.

### Is RM799 enough for a landing page?

Enough for most service SMEs. One clear page beats a big messy site every time. Upgrade only when you outgrow it, when you need to edit often or sell online.

### Should I stop ads and fix the website first?

If your page is broken or missing, yes. Every ringgit of ad spend landing on a bad page is wasted. Fix the page, then restart ads at a small budget and scale step by step.
