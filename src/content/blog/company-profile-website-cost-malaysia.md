## Why a company profile matters in Malaysia

A company profile is your first impression in a tender evaluation, a bank onboarding, or a corporate vendor meeting. Evaluators skim it to answer three questions: what do you do, have you done it before, and can I reach you. A clean, organised profile answers those in seconds — a generic one sends your PDF to the "maybe later" pile.

That's also why we sell the profile as a design product, not a data-entry job. Formatting your own Word document into a PDF is free; making it look established is a service.

## What makes a profile actually win

The design only gets you in the room — the content wins. A profile that performs covers: your capabilities and services, proof of work (past projects with names if possible), your team, and a single clear contact point. If you can add numbers (years operating, projects delivered), do.
