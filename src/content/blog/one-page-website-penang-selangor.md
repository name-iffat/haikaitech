Most SMEs in Penang and Selangor do not need a twenty-page corporate site. They need one page that answers three questions: what do you do, why should I trust you, and how do I contact you. That is exactly what a one-page website does — and when it fits, it is the fastest and cheapest route to a professional presence.

## When one page is enough

- Your business offers a small set of services (salon, workshop, clinic, contractor, kedai makan, homestay).
- Customers find you through Google, Facebook, TikTok or word of mouth — and just need somewhere credible to land.
- Enquiries close over WhatsApp, phone or walk-in, not through online checkout.
- You can summarise the business in five sections: hero, services, proof, about, contact.

Our introductory [RM799 starter](/website/) is built for exactly this shape: up to five sections, WhatsApp enquiry, basic SEO, a year of hosting and a standard domain.

## When you need more

- You sell products online and need carts, payments and delivery logic — that is an [ecommerce build](/services/ecommerce/), not a one-pager.
- Customers must book slots, choose variants or track orders — those workflows need quoting as [custom scope](/services/).
- You run paid ads at volume and need dedicated landing pages per campaign.
- Your catalogue genuinely needs browsing (dozens of products or services with detail).

A common middle path: launch the one-pager now, then extend it. A good build is structured so extra pages and features can be added later without starting over.

## The content you will need

One page still needs real content: your logo, service descriptions in your own words, prices or price ranges if you publish them, contact details, and photos you have permission to use. Most one-page delays come from waiting on these, not from the build itself.

## The honest bit

A one-page site will not outperform a bad offer, and no build guarantees enquiries or rankings. What it does is remove friction: every prospective customer who searches for you finds one clear, fast, credible page instead of a dead Facebook page or nothing at all.
