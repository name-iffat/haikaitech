## What moves the price

1. **Number of pages** — more pages mean more structure, design decisions, review and testing.
2. **Custom design complexity** — focused layout vs fully bespoke visual system.
3. **Content readiness** — approved copy and photos keep the build moving.
4. **Payments and integrations** — booking, ecommerce and gateways add workflows to verify.
5. **Ongoing costs** — renewals, plugins, transaction fees, identified separately.
