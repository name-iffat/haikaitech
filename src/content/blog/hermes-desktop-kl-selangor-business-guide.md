## If this is new to you

Hermes Desktop is an AI assistant app for your computer, built by Nous Research. You type requests in plain language, and it does computer work: drafting, organising, researching, remembering context across conversations.

It runs on your own machine, connects to a model provider of your choice, and can be given instructions about you and your business so its answers get more useful over time. It is not a chatbot on someone else's website — the setup, and the context you feed it, is yours.

## What a Klang Valley SME actually uses it for

- **Quotation and proposal drafts** — turn site-visit notes or a price list into a structured quote starting point. You review every figure before it goes out.
- **Supplier and tender research** — turn a pile of links, PDFs and price lists into a one-page brief before a meeting in PJ or Shah Alam.
- **Follow-up discipline** — draft polite payment reminders and project updates in your tone, keeping the pipeline warm without the awkwardness.
- **Business memory** — teach it your services, prices and standard answers once, and stop repeating yourself to every new enquiry.

Everything it drafts gets checked by a human before use. That rule is the whole difference between a useful assistant and an embarrassing incident.
